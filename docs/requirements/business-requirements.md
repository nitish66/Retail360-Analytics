**Version:** 1.0

---

# 1. Project Overview

Retail360 is a fictional multinational retail company operating both physical stores and an e-commerce platform.

The organization wants to replace static reports with a centralized cloud-based analytics platform that provides real-time insights for executives and business users.

The platform will be built using:

- Azure Blob Storage
- Azure Data Factory
- Azure SQL Database
- Power BI

---

# 2. Business Goals

The analytics platform should help management answer questions such as:

- How much revenue are we generating?
- Which products are the most profitable?
- Which customers generate the highest lifetime value?
- Which stores are underperforming?
- What inventory should be replenished?
- Which marketing campaigns deliver the best ROI?
- Are sales meeting budget targets?

---

# 3. Departments

The solution will support:

- Executive Leadership
- Sales
- Finance
- Marketing
- Inventory Management
- Supply Chain
- Human Resources

# 4. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

## Sales

- Total Revenue
- Gross Profit
- Net Profit
- Profit Margin %
- Average Order Value
- Sales Growth %

## Customer

- Customer Lifetime Value
- Customer Retention Rate
- New Customers
- Returning Customers

## Inventory

- Inventory Turnover
- Stock on Hand
- Reorder Quantity
- Dead Stock

## Marketing

- Campaign ROI
- Conversion Rate
- Cost per Lead

## Finance

- Budget vs Actual
- Operating Margin

---

# 5. Success Criteria

The solution should:

- Provide a single source of truth.
- Reduce manual reporting effort.
- Support self-service analytics.
- Enable near real-time reporting.
- Scale as the business grows.
